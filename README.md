## 💫 About Me

I’m a **Product Engineering Manager** at **PayItToday LLC** with a strong hands-on background as a **full-stack systems-focused engineer** in a **Ruby on Rails** environment.

My background spans **application engineering**, **data/platform engineering**, and **cloud/DevOps**, with hands-on experience building and supporting:

- production Rails applications
- product and platform development
- payment and remittance integrations
- backend systems and operational tooling
- data workflows with **Airflow**, **dbt**, and **Snowflake**
- cloud infrastructure and deployment workflows
- automation, reliability, and systems-focused solutions

I enjoy working across product, engineering, and operations — especially where **software**, **systems**, **data**, and **business needs** all intersect.

Outside of work, I’m an avid builder and hobbyist. I use **Linux as my daily driver**, run a personal **homelab**, and spend a lot of time working on self-hosted infrastructure, virtualization, storage, networking, and hardware projects.
